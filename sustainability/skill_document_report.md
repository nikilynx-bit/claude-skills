# Skill: Formal ESG Document Report Generator

## Objective
Transform the audited ESG data into a formal, professional corporate report suitable for stakeholders, auditors, and the sustainability committee.

## Instructions
1. **Structure the Document**: Use the following standardized corporate report structure:
   - **Title Page**: Report Name, Date, Prepared By.
   - **Executive Summary**: High-level overview of total emissions and overall compliance rates.
   - **Methodology**: Brief explanation of how travel data and CO2e were calculated.
   - **Carbon Footprint Analysis**: Breakdown of emissions by department and travel mode.
   - **Compliance & Data Quality**: Summary of missing data and its impact on reporting accuracy.
   - **Recommendations**: Actionable steps to improve data collection and compliance.
2. **Tone and Style**: Use formal, objective, and professional corporate language. 
3. **Data Integration**: Insert tables for departmental emission breakdowns and bulleted lists for key findings.

## Expected Output Format
A fully formatted Markdown document (ready to be exported to PDF/Word) with clear H1, H2, and H3 headers, professional tables, and a conclusive executive summary.
