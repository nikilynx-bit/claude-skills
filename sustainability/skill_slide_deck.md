# Skill: ESG Slide Deck Presentation Creator

## Objective
Distill the ESG travel data, carbon emissions, and compliance metrics into a high-impact slide deck for the executive board or town hall meeting.

## Instructions
1. **Slide Structuring**: Create a 6-to-8 slide presentation outline.
   - **Slide 1**: Title & Objective.
   - **Slide 2**: Executive Summary (Key metrics: Total CO2e, Compliance %).
   - **Slide 3**: Emissions Breakdown (Visual suggestion: Pie chart of emissions by Travel Mode).
   - **Slide 4**: Departmental Impact (Visual suggestion: Bar chart of CO2e by Department).
   - **Slide 5**: Data Quality & Challenges (Highlighting missing data issues).
   - **Slide 6**: The Compliance Gap (Highlighting non-completers).
   - **Slide 7**: Next Steps & Call to Action.
2. **Speaker Notes**: For each slide, write detailed speaker notes explaining the data context and what the presenter should emphasize.
3. **Visual Prompts**: Include bracketed suggestions for charts/graphics (e.g., `[Insert Bar Chart: Sales vs Engineering CO2e]`).

## Expected Output Format
A slide-by-slide text outline. Each slide should clearly separate the "Slide Content" (bullet points, chart placeholders) from the "Speaker Notes".
